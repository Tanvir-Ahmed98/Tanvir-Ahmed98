### 🚀 Key Projects

- **[Education Management ERP](https://app.academyims.com)** (Vue.js, Nuxt.js, PrimeVue, PrimeReact): multi-tenant ERP with 7+ modules covering fees, attendance, admissions, leave and ID card design, with server-side tables for large datasets.
- **UZO Sign** (React.js, TypeScript): multi-tenant document verification SaaS with role-based access, tenant-scoped data isolation and reusable form builders, document viewers and status trackers.
- **[Dimhum](https://dimhum.com)** (React.js, TypeScript, Vue.js, Nuxt.js): role-based crowdshipping platform with sender, receiver and traveller workflows, order creation, tracking and payments.
- **[Courier Service Platform](https://xpressintown.com)** (React.js, Pathao API): shipment creation, label generation and tracking, with validated booking and dispatch flows.
- **Vehicle Tracking System** (Raw PHP, MySQL, Debian Linux): IoT-based tracking with operational dashboards, deployed on a VPS with SSL/TLS.
- **Stock Management System** (Raw PHP, MySQL): inventory modules with tables, filters, pagination and full CRUD.
